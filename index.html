Foundation models · Multimodal LLMs

Haotian Zhang

Staff Research Scientist · NVIDIA Cosmos Lab

I develop foundation models and multimodal LLMs, with a focus on visual understanding, reasoning, and generation.

At NVIDIA Cosmos Lab, I work with Ming-Yu Liu on foundation models for physical AI. Previously at Apple AI/ML, I worked with Yinfei Yang on multimodal LLMs, including MM1, MM1.5, and the Ferret family. My earlier work at Microsoft Research includes GLIP and GLIPv2.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Washington, advised by Jenq-Neng Hwang, and my B.S. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
